# The House Sound, Elsewhere

## 24 instrumental score experiments

Preserve tactile wood, glass, strings, instrumental dialogue, and subtle electronics as family traits. Let each score choose its own rhythm, melodic density, and emotional scale. Regional entries are contemporary composed fusions, not historical reconstructions.

### Use

Paste a single code block into the Style prompt and enable Instrumental. No earlier prompt or custom model is required. Each prompt is below 1,000 characters.

Use v6 for the first pass, with suggested starting values of Weirdness 40 and Style Influence 85. For entries marked v6-wild, try Weirdness 60 and Style Influence 80. Keep Variety at 0 to preserve the written tags. These are audition starting points, not validated optimal settings. Leave other controls at default for the first pass.

Suno documents v6 and v6-wild as available on Pro and Premier, with the latter intended for more exploratory generation. The slider percentages here are recommendations, not documented presets. Tempo, meter, instrument isolation, and arrangement instructions remain generative targets, not guarantees.

## I. Strings, roots, and brass


### 01. The Clockwork String Band

Your mandolin–fiddle–cello–upright-bass quartet, with dobro and clean soul-funk guitar. The experiment is independent instrument motifs, quartet-only harmony, and a full-band payoff.

**Model:** v6 | **Style characters:** 836

```text
Instrumental progressive bluegrass, chamber electronica, acoustic post-minimalism, understated soul-funk; 92 BPM, relaxed 4/4 pocket. Mandolin carries a nimble repeating figure, fiddle sings an original answering melody, cello adds lyrical counterpoint, upright bass supplies woody syncopation; dobro slides between phrases and clean single-coil guitar adds muted ghost notes and warm blues double-stops. Sparse glass harmonics and barely audible analog air behind the acoustic players. Begin as a mandolin-cello duet; introduce individual motifs, open into four-part string harmony, strip to bass and guitar, then reunite the ensemble with interlocking melodies. Modal warmth, open fifths, supple microdynamics, audible pick and bow detail, wide clean mix. No vocals, choir, trap drums, EDM drops, breakneck picking or novelty hoedown.
```
